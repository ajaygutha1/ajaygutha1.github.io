Osmo

A personal assistant for one person.

Osmo helps its owner keep up with email, calendar and coursework. It reads the owner's mail to point out what needs attention, drafts and sends replies the owner has approved, and checks the owner's calendar when planning.

Osmo is not a public service. It runs on hardware its owner controls, and it connects only to Google accounts that belong to or are managed by its owner. There is no sign-up, and it cannot be used with anyone else's account.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages so it can summarize and triage them, and sends email from the owner's account when the owner asks or approves.
  • Google Calendar: reads events to plan around them, and creates or updates events when the owner asks.

How this data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
