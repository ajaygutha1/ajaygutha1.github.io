Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

Osmo is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Osmo does with data from Google accounts connected to it.

Data Osmo accesses

  • Gmail (read and send): message contents, headers, labels and attachments in connected accounts.
  • Google Calendar: events on connected calendars.

Only accounts that belong to or are managed by the owner are connected. Each one is connected by the owner signing in and granting access.

How the data is used

Only to do what the owner asks: summarize and prioritize email, draft and send messages the owner has approved or requested, and plan around the owner's schedule. Osmo does not use Google user data for advertising. It does not sell it. It does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Where it goes

Data is stored on computers the owner controls. To answer a request, Osmo may send the relevant content to a third-party AI model provider for processing. That processing only produces a response for the owner. Google user data is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

Google API Services

Osmo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked from any connected account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops Osmo from reading or sending anything further from that account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/cantsoar.
